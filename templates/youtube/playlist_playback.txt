Sega CD Constraint-Aware Video Codec: continuous playback from one single-speed CD, with automatic looping and no selection menu. The sources below play in order.

H40, centered in 320x224; 22.05 kHz mono IMA ADPCM decoded by the Sub CPU. Useful BODY rates below share the CD 1x ceiling of 150 KiB/s. VRAM tiles are reused through exact or close matches; new patterns are prefetched into spare console RAM against per-frame CD delivery deadlines. Palettes are trained per segment.

{items_en}

Captured in Genesis Plus GX with the Japanese Mega-CD BIOS. All movies have no diagnostic overlay. The complete startup and return to the first movie are retained. Native 320x224 is enlarged with nearest-neighbour scaling to 2048x1568 square pixels, preserving the H40 64:49 display shape.

Sega CD Constraint-Aware Video Codec：単速CD 1枚から、選択メニューなしで連続再生し、自動ループします。以下の素材を順に再生します。

H40、320x224の中央に配置。音声はSub CPUがdecodeする22.05 kHz mono IMA ADPCM。以下の有効BODY配送量はCD 1x上限150 KiB/s内です。VRAMのtileを完全一致または近似で再利用し、新しいpatternは各frameのCD配送期限に合わせて本体の空きRAMへ先読みします。paletteはsegmentごとに学習します。
